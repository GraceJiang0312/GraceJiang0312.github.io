Recent project · Stanford Probability for AI

2026 · Interactive tool

Word Detective

Which words give away AI-written text? Word Detective compares paired AI and human answers to the same questions and estimates, for every word, how likely it is to appear in each. The words with the biggest gap are the best clues.

The data covers four styles of Q&A: Reddit "Explain Like I'm 5", medical, financial, and CS/AI Wikipedia.

Worked example, W = text contains "super"
P(W | AI)    ≈ count(W | AI) / n    = 336 / 60,614 ≈ 0.0055
P(W | human) ≈ count(W | human) / n = 5 / 55,922   ≈ 0.0001
Strongest AI clues on "Explain Like I'm 5" count(AI) − count(human)

Same question, two answers: Why doesn't a candle smoke while burning, but does when you blow it out?

AIWhen a candle is burning, the flame is super hot and completely burns all the wax… It's like the flame eats it all up…
HumanWhen the flame is lit… that smoke is being burned. The smoke is vaporized wax.
